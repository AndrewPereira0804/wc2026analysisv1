# Scoring Rubric

## Inclusion Rule

Only controversial or materially important referee/VAR decisions are logged:
- Possible penalties
- Possible red cards
- DOGSO decisions
- Disallowed goals
- VAR checks
- Major no-calls/no-cards

Routine fouls, ordinary yellows, or incidents with no real match impact are not logged.

## Impact Score

+4 = Game winning decision to favored team
+3 = major benefit to favored team
+2 = meaningful benefit to favored team
+1 = minor benefit to favored team
0 = no meaningful effect
-1 = minor benefit to underdog
-2 = meaningful benefit to underdog
-3 = major benefit to underdog
-4 = Game winning decision to underdog

## Standard Fit
On a scale of -1 to 1
+1 = Much stricter call than expected
0 = Consistent with how the referee managed the game
-1 = Much more lenient call (or lack thereof) than expected

## Call Correctness

0.0 = clearly wrong call  
0.5 = debatable  
1.0 = clearly correct call  

## Confidence
On a scale of 0-1
0.0 = I am very uncertain in my assessment 
1.0 = I am very certain in my assessment
